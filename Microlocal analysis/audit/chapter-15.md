# Chapter 15, the families index theorem

## Findings

1. **The joint symbol kernel contains compact operators with nonsmooth kernels.** The kernel of the joint symbol map is $\Psi_{\rm pt}^{-1,-1}$. For example, compress a classical base operator of exact order $-1$ by a rank-one smoothing fibre projection. Its two $(0,0)$ symbols vanish, but its kernel is singular along the base diagonal. The lower orders suffice for the intended index argument. The inclusions of ordinary and fibre operators used in the proof also require the classical convention from Chapter 14. [Preliminaries, lines 250–271](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L250-L271)

2. **The Clifford-matrix homotopy notation inserts the cutoff twice.** In the paragraph following the first homotopy, the notation $m(1-t\chi_2,\chi_2)$ is the expression with $p,a$ replaced by $\gamma^*p,\alpha$ in the corresponding entries. Using $\chi_2\alpha$ there inserts an extra factor. The displayed matrix implements the desired homotopy. When $M=B$, the base cosphere of the relative fibration is empty and $A$ is a bundle map. Remark 15.2.6 states the right conclusion. In this case the main homotopy relies on invertibility of the $P$ symbol alone. [Proof, lines 700–750](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L700-L750)

3. **The excision proof needs a representative defined on the region where it evaluates $a$.** A triple initially provides $a$ only outside its compact exceptional set. The block normal form uses it in the interior. First extend by cutoff from an exterior neighbourhood, possibly enlarging the compact set, and then perform the block deformation. [Excision proof, lines 841–874](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L841-L874)

4. **The open-set restriction used in the product formula need not be a compactly supported K-class.** If the support of the second class reaches the boundary of the open set, the restricted triple need not be invertible outside a compact subset of that open set. Use the representative restricted to the open set only inside the Clifford product, whose support is controlled by the first class. Likewise, in the sphere-embedding proof choose the connection globally on the compactified fibration and restrict it to the stereographic open set. A general splitting on the open set need not extend at infinity. [Excision and product, lines 950–1030](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L785-L814) · [Sphere proof, lines 1205–1220](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L1205-L1220)

5. **Both fibre-first integration formulas have the wrong commutation sign with $d$.** With the printed convention $\mu=dt\wedge\mu_1+\mu_2$, the $dt$-coefficient of $d\mu$ is $-d_X\mu_1+\partial_t\mu_2$. The second term integrates to zero on a circle or for compact support in the line, so integration anticommutes with $d$. The cohomological constructions still work because exact forms map to exact forms. [Circle, lines 1481–1495](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L1481-L1495) · [Line, lines 1805–1816](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L1805-L1816)

6. **Even matrix-valued forms need not commute.** In the tensor-connection proof, the two curvature terms commute because they act in different tensor factors and have even degree. In the clutching proof, the two terms commute because they are built from powers of the same matrix-valued $1$-form and scalar coefficients. Use these reasons where the text invokes even degree by itself. [Tensor connection, lines 1335–1352](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L1335-L1352) · [Clutching, lines 1827–1841](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L1827-L1841)

7. **The relative integration formula needs orientation, and the boundary-current expression needs a convention.** Use an oriented $X$ for the scalar pairing with its relative fundamental class. If the current is interpreted on a double and $x$ increases inward, write $1_{x>0}\mu+\delta(x)dx\wedge\nu$ after extension by zero. Then its derivative has boundary term $\delta(x)dx\wedge(\iota^*\mu-d\nu)$. This determines the signs from the convention already stated in the chapter. [Relative pairing, lines 1593–1610](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L1593-L1610) · [Current, lines 1749–1768](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/14-families.tex#L1749-L1768)

## Checks by argument

- **K-theory preliminaries and analytic index.** Checked the Clifford signs, support of the product, associativity under the graded identification, the finite-dimensional stabilization, the smooth kernel-bundle frames, independence of auxiliary sections, and the passage from symbols to classes. Whitney embedding, elliptic Fredholm theory, Bott periodicity and ordinary K-theory facts are used as cited inputs. Positive-rank finite-dimensional adjustments use compact fibres and base. Zero-dimensional fibres should be handled as vector bundles where the proof calls for infinitely many fibre functions.
- **Multiplicativity.** Checked that the block decomposition separates the index-one summand and the invertible complement. Checked that $\chi_1 p$ and $\chi_2\alpha$ are well-defined on complementary cosphere regions and that the positive lower bound for the Clifford matrix follows from the two terms in its squared norm. The compressed homotopy and ordinary endpoint have the asserted two symbols, with Chapter 14’s classical convention.
- **Excision, normalization and reduction to the sphere.** Checked the support of the transplanted kernel and how the two operators identify their null spaces and cokernels. Checked fibre-cotangent splittings on the tubular neighbourhood, the trivial Bott model and its product index, and the compact-support diagrams. The two representative/splitting details are recorded above. The relative compactification used for the product-type calculus agrees with Appendix B’s polar coordinates.
- **Chern character and the cohomological formula.** Checked Bianchi and trace identities, transgression, the block-rotation proof of odd additivity, relative cocycles, the sign of the boundary term, and the clutching factorial and $(2\pi i)^{-k-1}$ factor. Checked the fibre-orientation convention against the $(-1)^n$ in the formula. Theorem 5.1 in Atiyah–Singer IV is the cited cohomological formula, and the Riemann–Roch/Thom-character identity is an external input. [Atiyah–Singer IV, §5](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/as4.pdf#page=16)
- **Exercises and solutions.** Checked the exterior-product relations and block flip, the CP¹ Chern form, the Bott clutching integral, odd additivity by homotopy, naturality of stabilization under pullback, and the determinant winding calculation. The last two use proper pullback on the vertical cotangent bundles and the fixed normalization $\operatorname{ind}(b_1)=1$. The winding and Chern-character signs agree in the coordinate orientation used in the text.
