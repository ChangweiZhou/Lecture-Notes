# Chapter 14, §§14.1–14.4 and Exercises 14.1–14.4

1. **Local triviality is componentwise.** Part (2) of Theorem 14.1.2 and the accompanying caption should say $Z_b\times U_b$ unless the base is connected or the fibre types on its components are known to agree. For a base of two points, take one fibre to be a circle and the other a disjoint union of two circles. The flow proof gives the required local trivializations. [Theorem, lines 68–85](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/13-product.tex#L68-L85)

2. **The homogeneous principal symbol sequence is classical.** Definition 14.2.1 uses the general conormal class from Chapter 4. Proposition 14.2.3(3) then assigns it a homogeneous symbol, which the general ordinary calculus of Chapter 5 does not do. A constant family with a symbol $\sin(\log\langle\zeta\rangle)$ distinguishes the two classes. Either state the homogeneous sequence for the classical subclass or use the ordinary order quotient for the general class. The subsequent index-bundle statements involving homogeneous symbols should specify that subclass or refer to the ordinary quotient. [Definition, lines 282–298](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/13-product.tex#L282-L298) · [Proposition, lines 348–383](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/13-product.tex#L348-L383)

3. **Kernel support alone does not imply commutation with base multipliers.** In the proof of Proposition 14.2.3 a derivative of the base delta distribution would have the same support but need not commute. Use the definition's pushforward from the fibre product, which gives the undifferentiated delta in the base, or use the local family form. In the index-bundle proof the dimensions asserted constant are locally constant on $B$; the smooth bundle proof already works componentwise. [Commutation, lines 385–391](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/13-product.tex#L385-L391) · [Index bundle, lines 605–619](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/13-product.tex#L605-L619)

4. **The motivating answer needs the product weight.** Blowing up changes $(x^2+|v|^2)^{\mu/2}$ to $R^\mu$, which still fails to be smooth at $R=0$ for generic $\mu$. The lifted $\langle w\rangle^{-\mu}\langle\zeta\rangle^\mu$ becomes smooth after dividing by the front-face power $R^\mu$. Equivalently, $\rho_W^\mu\langle\zeta\rangle^\mu=1$ is smooth in the product calculus. Proposition 14.4.3(ii) uses $1/|\eta|$, its second listed entry, as the local defining function. [Motivation, lines 652–666](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/13-product.tex#L652-L666) · [Coordinates, lines 745–769](https://github.com/ChangweiZhou/Lecture-Notes/blob/5c20ed12c559cbbbd6934f2821086a050979953b/Microlocal%20analysis/chapters/13-product.tex#L745-L769)
