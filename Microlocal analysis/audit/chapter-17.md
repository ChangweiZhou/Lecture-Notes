## Findings

1. **Lemma 17.2.3, lines 188–194. The correction in holomorphic division uses the wrong power of the defining function.** In a local representation $\operatorname{Op}(\rho^{-\mu(s)}b(s))+R(s)$, subtract $\operatorname{Op}(\rho^{-\mu(s)}b(s_0))$ from the symbol operator and add it to $R(s)$. The subtracted family is smoothing since $b(s_0)$ is flat. Both corrected terms then vanish at $s_0$. The printed extra $\rho^{\mu(s_0)}$ generally prevents this, and the smoothing family added to the old remainder need not itself vanish at $s_0$. This lemma is used for the residue and trace-defect formulas.
2. **Proposition 17.3.6, lines 411–435. The order-$m-1$ symbol of the derivation is missing a matrix commutator.** With $Q=\langle D\rangle$, $E(s)=Q^s(I+sQ^{-1}C)$, and $A=B$ constant with $[C,B]\ne0$, the Poisson bracket vanishes and $D_EA=Q^{-1}[C,B]$. Add the commutator of the differentiated lower coefficient of $E(s)$ with the leading symbol of $A$. The order drop and the chosen scalar circle regularizer remain valid.
3. **Proposition 17.3.1, lines 295–317. The invariant density is on the base after sphere integration.** The pointwise density from the degree-$-d$ term of a local left symbol need not descend to $S^*Z$. For $d=2$ conjugate a scalar order-$-1$ operator with principal symbol $\xi_1/|\xi|^2$ locally by $e^f$. The order-$-2$ term acquires a nonzero multiple of $\partial_{\xi_1}(\xi_1/|\xi|^2)$ when $f=x_1$ locally, and this term integrates to zero on the circle. The residue functional and the cosphere integral in a chosen representation are valid. The argument with a radial closed form proves independence of the sphere section for a fixed homogeneous term.
4. **Lemma 17.1.2, lines 119–125. The smoothing approximation needs the larger symbol space.** With the chapter's definition of one-step classical order, a noninteger $\delta>0$ does not put a classical symbol of order $m_1$ in the classical space of order $m_1+\delta$. Use the ordinary Hörmander seminorms of order $\Re m_1+\delta$, in which frequency cutoffs do converge. The trace-class bound uses only estimates in that space.
5. **§17.4–17.5, lines 486–498 and 676–680. Clarify the full-symbol variable, rank, and winding sign.** The series for $D_EA$ must be an asymptotic expansion in terms of the full symbol of $A$. For example an order-$-1$ summand of an order-zero $A$ can contribute to order $-2$. The regularized trace of $\Pi\otimes I_N$ is $N/2$; $1/2$ is the scalar result. The Toeplitz index in (17.5.1) is the negative winding, so the winding integer in the determinant paragraph is its negative when applied to a loop of symbols.
6. **§17.6, lines 757–767. Distinguish the full symbol from the principal symbol in regularizer dependence.** For general order-zero $D(E,E')$, the residue sees the next coefficient of $Q$ as well as that of $D$. For example use $D=\Pi$, and on an interval in the parameter take $B(t)=\Pi+tQ_{-1}$ for a positive-frequency elliptic order-$-1$ multiplier $Q_{-1}$ of nonzero residue. Its principal symbol stays the identity while the regularizer change at $t=0$ is $\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathrm R}(Q_{-1})dt$. The order-$-1$ restriction in Exercise 17.3 correctly gives a principal-symbol formula.
7. **§17.9, lines 1308–1314. Add the fibre-smoothing step to the unitary doubling proof.** Off-diagonal blocks between positive and negative circle frequencies must be fibre smoothing to all orders to compose in one variable with the reflection. Vanishing of the single principal symbol would only lower one order. For a classical symbol use opposite frequency support and rapid Fourier decay in the circle base variable to get all orders of fibre smoothing, uniformly in the remaining symbol parameters.
8. **§17.10, lines 1430–1460. State orientation hypotheses for the integral pairing.** The long exact sequence needs fibre orientation for the push-forward. The numerical integral pairing also needs $Z$ and $Y$ oriented with the boundary orientation on $\partial Z$ agreeing with base followed by fibre. Without those hypotheses, the two integrals need not exist as stated.
