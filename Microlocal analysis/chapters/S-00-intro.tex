\chapter{Solutions to the exercises}\label{ch:solutions}

This appendix works out every exercise in the book, chapter by chapter, in the
order in which the exercises appear. Each solution begins with a reference to the
exercise it answers; the exercise number is a link. Solutions use only material
from the chapter in which the exercise appears and from earlier chapters, unless
the exercise itself points elsewhere. Where an exercise has several parts, the
parts are answered in order. Hints given in the statements are followed where
they are helpful, but the solutions are complete without them.

Readers are encouraged to attempt each exercise before reading its solution:
most solutions are short once the right idea is found, and the Discussion blocks in
the chapters often point to that idea.
